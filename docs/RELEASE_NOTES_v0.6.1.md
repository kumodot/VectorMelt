## VectorMelt v0.6.1

- **Multi selection**: *Shift* / *Ctrl* click layers on the canvas or in the layer list, or drag a box on empty canvas (*Shift* adds). *Ctrl+A* selects all, *Esc* clears. Move, arrow nudge, scale and rotate act on the whole selection; the group gizmo scales and rotates around the group centre. *Ctrl+D* / *Delete* work on every selected layer. *Shift* while moving locks the axis.
- **Pan**: middle drag, right drag or *Space* + drag (left drag on empty canvas is box select now).
- **New shapes**: *Triangle*, *Cross*, *Arrow*. Exact distance fields; *Corner radius* rounds only the convex corners and keeps the straight edges in place.
- Shape buttons are icons. *Box* starts square.

Run: open `VectorMelt_v0.6.1.html`, or online at https://kumodot.github.io/VectorMelt/

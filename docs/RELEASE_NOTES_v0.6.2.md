## VectorMelt v0.6.2

- **Line arrow**: *Arrow* is now a stroked arrow (shaft plus two head strokes, round caps) with *Length*, *Thickness*, *Head size* and *Head angle*. *Arrow style* switches to the filled *Block* arrow; arrows in older project files stay *Block*.
- Also in the 0.6.x line: multi selection (Shift / Ctrl click, box select, Ctrl+A, group move / scale / rotate), *Triangle* and *Cross* shapes, icon buttons for the shapes.

Run: open `VectorMelt_v0.6.2.html`, or online at https://kumodot.github.io/VectorMelt/

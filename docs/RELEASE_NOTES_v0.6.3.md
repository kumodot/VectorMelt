## VectorMelt v0.6.3

- **Export feedback**: *Export SVG* and *Export PNG* now show a busy overlay with a spinner while they work, the button is disabled until it finishes, and a toast confirms the saved file (pixel size, MB, time) or shows the error. Before, a big PNG with exact fillets froze the page for a few seconds with no sign of life.

Run: open `VectorMelt_v0.6.3.html`, or online at https://kumodot.github.io/VectorMelt/

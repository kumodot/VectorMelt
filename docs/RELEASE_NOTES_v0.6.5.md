## VectorMelt v0.6.5

- **Fast edit** (Preview, on by default): while you drag a layer, the gizmo or a shape slider, or nudge with the arrows, the exact *Profile* / fillets are skipped and the live soft blend is shown. The exact result is rebuilt when you let go, so moving things stays smooth with many layers.
- **Layer Profile** master switch (*Exact fillets* panel): turns *Profile* off for all layers at once without losing their values. Saved with the project.

Run: open `VectorMelt_v0.6.5.html`, or online at https://kumodot.github.io/VectorMelt/

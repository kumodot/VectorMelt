## VectorMelt v0.6.0

- **Exact Profile**: *Profile* above 0 on a *Round* layer now gives a true rolling ball fillet at that layer's junction (radius 0.6 x *Blend*). Clean arcs on text, images and SVG; the letters' own corners are left alone. No more noise or spikes when *Profile* goes up.
- **Fillet circles**: construction circles in the overlay (ball, centre, tangent points), toggle in *Preview*.
- The *Profile* preview updates right after each change (it is computed on a grid, like *Exact fillets*).

Run: open `VectorMelt_v0.6.0.html`, or online at https://kumodot.github.io/VectorMelt/
